Sell your jewelry with confidence — private, transparent, paid on the spot

Inspired by what South Florida sellers expect most: a private consultation, an evaluation explained step-by-step, and a pressure-free offer. Whether it's one diamond ring or an entire jewelry box from an estate, we treat every piece with the same care.

What we purchase: fine jewelry in 14kt and 18kt gold and platinum set with diamonds and precious gemstones — necklaces, bracelets, earrings, rings, pendants and brooches. We have special interest in estate and antique jewelry, modern designer pieces, loose diamonds from half a carat (with premiums for 2 carats and up), and antique cuts such as Old Mine and Rose Cut.

  • White and fancy colored diamonds, certified or uncertified
  • Gold jewelry 10K–24K — including outdated, broken or scrap pieces valued for intrinsic worth, with more for designer or antique value
  • Platinum and sterling silver jewelry and flatware
  • Colored gemstones — emerald, ruby, sapphire, jade, coral and pearls
  • Single pieces, full collections, and estate jewelry lots

Designer names we actively seek include many of the houses collectors look for. If you have a signed piece, bring any box, papers, or prior appraisal — it can help confirm provenance and support value.

Designer brands: Tiffany & Co. · Cartier · Van Cleef & Arpels · Bulgari · David Yurman · David Webb · Chanel · Boucheron · Buccellati · Georg Jensen · Fabergé · Roberto Coin

Luxury watches: we buy Rolex, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Vacheron Constantin, Cartier, Omega, Breitling, Chopard, Hublot, Jaeger-LeCoultre, Panerai and other fine Swiss timepieces — pre-owned, vintage or new. Original box and papers are a plus; condition, materials, movement and current demand all factor into the offer.

How selling jewelry works here

No pressure, no obligation — just a clear number and the reasoning behind it.

1. Bring what you have — One piece or many. Any certificates, appraisals, boxes or brand paperwork help, but they're not required.

2. Private evaluation — We examine stones, metal, maker, condition and current market demand — right in front of you.

3. We explain the value — You'll hear how we arrived at the figure, including metal weight, stone quality (the 4Cs for diamonds), and comparable sales.

4. Offer & prompt payment — If you accept, we pay promptly and provide a receipt. Take time to decide if you prefer — the conversation stays pressure-free.

Not local or prefer to start remotely? Call us, describe the piece or send photos through the form below, and we'll give you a ballpark before you visit. House calls available across Boca Raton and Palm Beach County by appointment.

Before you call

Is the first appraisal really free?

Yes. The first step is a free informal appraisal: we review your item or estate, give you an estimated value based on the current market, and explain your selling options — with no obligation.

Do I need a full estate to work with you?

Not at all. We buy and consign single items — a painting, a silver service, a piece of jewelry — as well as complete estates and entire collections.

What's the difference between selling and consigning?

Selling to us outright is the fastest route — you get paid without waiting. Consignment can earn more for desirable pieces, since your items are offered to our network of collectors and buyers. We'll recommend the best route for each piece.

Do you provide certified appraisals?

Yes. In addition to free informal appraisals, we provide certified appraisals suitable for federal estate tax filings and insurance coverage.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Boca Raton and work with families across South Florida, from Vero Beach down to Miami — including Deerfield Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Delray Beach, and Palm Beach County.

Do you buy jewelry and watches on their own, without an estate?

Yes — every day. We buy single pieces and full collections of fine jewelry, diamond jewelry, gold and platinum pieces, and luxury watches such as Rolex, Patek Philippe, Cartier and Omega. Bring any certificates, boxes or prior appraisals you have; they help, but they're not required for an evaluation.

What kind of jewelry do you pay the most for?

Diamond jewelry (especially 1 carat and up), signed designer pieces, antique and estate jewelry in gold or platinum, and jewelry with fine colored gemstones tend to bring the strongest offers. Even broken or outdated gold is purchased for its intrinsic value — and if a piece has designer or antique significance, we value that above melt.

How does consignment work?

We evaluate your pieces, photograph and present them to collectors, dealers and designers. You approve the plan before anything is listed. After a sale, we handle payment, pickup or shipping, and settlement — and anything unsold can be returned, re-offered, or purchased directly.

Do you only buy outright, or can I consign?

SoBe Antiques buys outright for immediate payment, sells by private sale and estate sale, and consigns property to collectors and private buyers. At your free appraisal we'll recommend — piece by piece — which route is likely to net you the most.

Do you work with attorneys, banks, and trust departments?

Yes. In addition to private sellers and families, we regularly assist attorneys, trust departments, banks, insurance companies, executors, dealers, and interior designers with appraisals, consignments, and complete estate sales — with clear documentation at every step.

Will you leave the home clean afterwards?

Yes — that's part of the job. Once items are sold or removed, we leave the property in broom-clean condition, ready for closing, listing, or its next occupants.
